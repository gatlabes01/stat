Danny Trejo says he tried to back out of a movie about ICE and immigration enforcement ... but his plans were derailed when he was threatened with a lawsuit.

We got the legendary actor ... and our photog asked him about the movie "Pawn Shop," produced by Ben Shapiro's company. .

Danny tells us, "I tried [to back out], and they said no … they'd sue the hell out of me.".

So Danny pivoted. He says he just "didn't say what they wanted me to say" … alluding to him changing the lines in the script. .

Danny tells us he eventually finished the film, noting he was under contract.

The actor also praised TMZ's Harvey Levin for his support. .

As TMZ previously reported, Danny spoke out about the backlash he received after people found out he was working on a movie about ICE. .

Danny previously told TMZ, "I am a proud Mexican who has always been and will always be a supporter of my community. I am also obviously pro-immigrant. I am not pro-ICE.".

"I know where I come from and I know our struggles," he said. .
