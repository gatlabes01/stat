10 Stunning Partners of Elite Athletes

These Stunning Partners of World’s Elite Athletes are Turning heads both on & off field for their partners and we selected Top 10 of them for you to choose:

 

 

Tom Brady & Irina Shayk

The legendary former American football quarterback Tom Brady, known for his unparalleled skill on the field, has also gained attention for his amorous pursuits. Tom’s romantic life took an interesting turn after his divorce from Super Model Gisele Bundchen in 2022 when he started seeing the gorgeous Irina Shayk. Their dating  began in early 2023 at a charming Italian wedding, where a unique bond emerged.

Tom and Irina are enjoying their time together without marriage, and for the time being, their relationship is getting serious so we’ll see what the future holds

Eli Manning & Abby McGrew

Prepare to be fascinated with Eli Manning and Abby McGrew’s surprising love tale—a pair whose long marriage has gone largely unnoticed. When they were only college sweethearts, their love story started in the spring of 2002 at the University of Mississippi. In 2008, they tied the knot in a surprise wedding ceremony that brought many people to tears.

In January 2020, Eli, the quarterback for the NFL for an incredible 16 years, announced his retirement. He couldn’t resist thanking his wife and their four kids in his goodbye speech for being his steadfast supporters during his brilliant football career.

Julian Edelman & Daiane Sodre

In the world of American football, Julian Edelman is a true superstar. He earned a name for himself as a wide receiver with the New England Patriots for 12 incredible seasons. However, Edelman’s path to NFL fame was not like any other. At Kent State University, he was a gifted quarterback before he started collecting passes from Tom Brady. NFL scouts were drawn to his extraordinary skills, which ultimately resulted in his selection in the seventh round of the 2009 NFL Draft.

Julian hung up his professional cleats in ‘21 and set out for new adventures, which gave him more time to work on his romance with the captivating Daiane Sodre.

LeBron James & Savannah James

Throughout his incredible NBA career, LeBron James—who is frequently referred to as King James—has continuously highlighted the significant impact of his loyal partner, Savannah. Since their first meeting at a nearby high school football game, Savannah has been LeBron’s rock—not just as a devoted philanthropist but also as a powerful businesswoman.

In 2013, the couple’s three-day, extravagant wedding in San Diego came to an end with 200 guests, including well-known people like Gabrielle Union and Dwyane Wade, who gave the event a dash of star appeal.

 

Jalen Brunson & Ali Marks

Jalen Brunson, who is well-known for his skill on the basketball court, rose to prominence at Villanova University while playing collegiate basketball. A tribute to his extraordinary abilities, he even won the coveted title of National Player of the Year in 2014 as a junior. Brunson didn’t stop there; in 2018, he helped his squad win two national championships. Jalen is having success outside of the basketball court in his personal life as well.

 

Russell Wilson & Ciara

When Russell Wilson persuaded his wife Ciara to adopt his surname, he must have felt he had accomplished something remarkable. But since she doesn’t use a last name, the joke was on him. Their marriage is still going strong, though. Four years together might not seem like much, but considering his first marriage to Ashton Meem lasted barely two years, it is a huge accomplishment. The couple made the announcement that they were expecting their second child in January 2020.

 

 

Magic Johnson & Cookie Kelly

 

Magic Johnson is a well-known individual who has found success in the business world as well as on the basketball court. He has been with his wife, Cookie Kelly, since their undergraduate days. They were married in 1991 after more than 30 years of dating punctuated by a few setbacks, such as infidelity and breakups.

Charles Wade Barkley and Maureen Barkley

Charles Barkley’s basketball career is nothing short of legendary, having been selected to the All-NBA Team eleven times and making an astounding eleven NBA All-Star appearances. Unquestionably one of the top 50 players in NBA history, he is still in high demand as an analyst for “Inside the NBA.” Barkley is well-known for his magnetism on TV, but he also fiercely guards his personal life. Nonetheless, his long-lasting marriage to Maureen is a well-known truth. His time with the Philadelphia 76ers was when their romance began, and in 1989 they tied the knot, starting a journey that has now lasted for several decades.

Luka Doncic & Anamaria

This new Basketball star Luka Doncic of the Mavs is dating fashion model and social media personality Anamaria Goltes. The pair had reportedly been friends since their childhood years and began dating in 2016. The pair had a little breakup due to a disagreement, but they made up in 2018 and were spotted together once more.

Luka and Anamaria frequently post, like, and comment on social media, publicly demonstrating their support for one another. Despite their frequent travel due to their demanding professional schedules, they have established a home base in Texas and spend a lot of time there.

Michael Jordan & Yvette Prieto

Most people agree that Michael Jordan is among the all-time best basketball players. Shortly after divorcing his first wife in 2008, he started dating Cuban beauty Yvette Prieto. Soon after, the couple moved in together. In 2013, they had their opulent wedding in Palm Beach, Florida, which was attended by celebrities including Spike Lee and Tiger Woods. Usher also sang at the celebration. Following the arrival of their twin children in 2014, the couple has chosen to keep their personal life secret.
