Taylor Swift's Former Music Teacher Speaks Out After Vanity Fair Comments

Taylor Swift's former music teacher, who spoke about the singer to Vanity Fair, says she thinks highly of Taylor … despite what the article shared. .

Sharon Luyben, who taught at Wyomissing Area Junior/Senior High for three decades, tells TMZ ... "All I said were positive things." .

She tells us Taylor was a "fabulous student" and adds, "I was also a neighbor and friend with her parents who were very nice and active in our community.".

Vanity Fair did not use quotes from Sharon speaking highly of Taylor … instead, it used only one quote from her denying Taylor's claim that people didn't want to sit with her at lunch … and would move away from her. .

Sharon told the reporter students had assigned seating at lunch … and, as such, Taylor's claim didn't make sense. Another student backed up the claim … while a second student the outlet spoke to wasn't sure. .

The music teacher isn't the only one clearing the air after the article..

Taylor's former classmate, Arielle Peppers, told us she wasn't trying to throw shade when she recalled a story about Taylor's expensive Ugg boots.

As you know, Vanity Fair dropped a scathing profile of Taylor on Monday ... taking aim at her carefully cultivated image, personal relationships, and alleged industry feuds ... though she's reportedly not letting it get to her.
