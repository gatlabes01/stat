Khloe Kardashian's former best friend, Malika Haqq, isn't holding back amid their bitter feud ... she's speaking out and sending a clear message.

She ended with a warning, "Play with someone else. My silence is your gift. But keep poking the bear.".

Khloe and Malika have been friends for decades … and Malika and her sister regularly appeared on "Keeping Up with the Kardashians" and "The Kardashians" … along with Khloe's various spin-off shows.&nbsp;.
