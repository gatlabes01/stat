7 Incredible Must See Celeb Mansions

When it comes to luxurious mansions no body does it better than these world’s top celebrities, whether it be pool in the house or a huge backyard with tennis courts these 7 celebs mansion are a must see:

 

CELINE DION – Las Vegas Mansion

The singer of the song “My Heart Will Go On” and her three kids, who she had with her late husband René Angelil, share a $1.2 million mansion. The lavish estate offers access to a semi-private golf course, three roomy bedrooms, and a large outdoor area with a pool and private bar.

Stainless steel fixtures and glass-fronted cupboards abound in Celine’s stylish white kitchen. Another of her rooms has an eye-catching all-white pattern complete with luxurious draperies, a high-shine white table, leather tub chairs, and candles fit for any celebration. The space feels regal because of the double-height elaborate doors and the beautiful cushioned chair, which contrast with the ultramodern bookshelves.

 

Cher – Malibu

With a seven-decade career, Cher is one of the most popular musicians and actress in the world. She is rightfully referred to as the “Goddess of Pop” by many. Her accomplishment in a range of mediums, such as music, movies, and television, is well known. Her money has been wisely spent, as she has bought properties on both the eastern and western coasts but this Malibu mansion costs over  !

Most of them are previously sold by her. She appears to have retained her most well-known residence, an Italian Renaissance-style mansion perched on a Malibu hill. There’s an infinity pool, multiple verandas, and a tennis court (which Cher especially loves).

 

Diana Ross – Connecticut

Diana Ross, a popular singer and artist from the era of the 1970s owns this huge $14.4 million mansion , continues to win folks over. Her beautiful mansion is her most valuable possession, even more than all the other things she has. Her affluent Greenwich, Connecticut home is furnished with every comfort and amenity imaginable.

 

Her magnificent estate covers 5 acres. The home is situated near Belle Haven and was constructed around 1931. This old-style house has two apartments above the garage, a tennis court, a pool, and a hot tub. In addition, it features five fireplaces, six bathrooms, and eleven bedrooms. This property, which spans over 12,550 square feet, is amazing.

 

Clint Eastwood- California Manor

This $5 million mansion belongs to the legendary famed cowboy American actor, filmmaker, producer, and composer Clinton Eastwood rose to prominence in the Western television series Rawhide. His role in the Dollars Trilogy film brought him widespread recognition. Aside from that, in the late 1990s, Eastwood embodied the perfect masculinity.

Having turned twenty-two acres of property into a ranch, he is a happy owner. His ranch’s shiny structures house thirty-one magnificent guest rooms, a remodeled restaurant and bar, and the most breathtaking vistas in California. The charming community of Carmel is home to this magnificent property. Numerous livestock and his other residence can be seen on this lush estate.

 

Goldie Hawn & Kurt Russell – California Manor

 

This $6.9 million mansion belongs to Goldie Hawn who is an actress of prominence who rose to stardom with Rowan & Martin’s Laugh-In, while partner Kurt Russell is an American actor most recognized for Guardians of the Galaxy. The couple that will always be in love own a stunning property in Calif. The estate, which is situated in the Palisades Riviera, was constructed around 1951 and has subsequently undergone improvement.

The huge living area has French doors that go out to the yard and see a skylight. The first-floor master suite and a guest suite with a vaulted ceiling and stairs leading to a separate loft area are two of the house’s five bedrooms. In addition, there’s a pool with a spa, media room, and gymnasium.

 

Nicole Kidman – Nashville Mansion

Popular Australian actress Nicole Kidman was listed as one of the world’s top one hundred individuals by Time magazine. Kidman has been included among the highest-paid actresses in the world. The actress is partial to extravagant, luxurious homes. She owns properties in West Chelsea, Beverly Hills, and Manhattan, but her Nashville estate is where she wants to remain.

This home was purchased by her for $3.47 million in 2007. The 12,500 square foot estate has seven bedrooms, eight complete bathrooms, and two half bathrooms. The estate has a tennis court and an outdoor pool to give even more beauty. It’s a quite bargain for the huge amount of square footage!
