Russell Brand got some family time behind bars on Thanksgiving Eve ... his wife Laura Brand brought their kids to visit him in prison in England.

The family visit marks the first time Laura's been publicly photographed visiting Russell since she addressed the controversy surrounding a message she shared from her husband behind bars.

As we reported ... British officials launched an investigation after Laura read a message from Russell on her "Sunday Service" YouTube show earlier this month, in which he maintained his innocence despite his conviction.

Laura addressed the controversy, acknowledging the attention her previous episode received and pledging to respect the prison's rules moving forward.

British authorities prohibit inmates from issuing statements that could cause distress to their victims, including using others to publicly relay their messages.

Russell is currently serving a 10-and-a-half-year prison sentence after being convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault.
