Reality star Khadijah Haqq revealed her modest monthly income ... as she fights her estranged husband Bobby McCray for child and spousal support in their divorce, TMZ has learned.&nbsp;.

Per court docs, Khadijah said her average monthly income for 2026 is $5,241. The filing said she earns another $437 a month in residuals. She said her ex earned around $18K a month in income and other benefits.&nbsp;.

Khadijah listed that she had $11K in the bank.

The Hulu reality star listed $13K in monthly expenses … $4K in rent, $1,900 on groceries, $400 on eating out, $100 on laundry, $550 on clothes, $2,600 on education, $500 on entertainment and $1,100 on various other expenses.&nbsp;.

She said she owes $2K on her Best Buy credit card, $11K on her Capital One credit card, $30K to her sister for an unpaid loan and another $80K loan received from a “friend.”&nbsp;.

As TMZ first reported, Khadijah and her ex and a former NFL star are still working out a deal over support and custody.&nbsp;.

Khadijah said she earned $25K per episode for the Kardashian-produced reality show "The Girls," which aired on Hulu. She said she only earned a total of $150K for the project.&nbsp;.

She asked the court to award $7K a month in child and spousal support ... plus another $100K to cover her legal bills in the case.&nbsp;.
