"1000-Lb. Sisters" alum Michael Halterman is in big trouble with the law ... he's been arrested for allegedly assaulting a child in Kentucky, TMZ has learned.

Police have not yet released details on what led to Halterman's arrest.

As you may know ... Halterman tied the knot with Slaton in 2017 ... but they got divorced in 2023.

The ex-couple shares 2 sons -- Glenn Allen, 4, and Gage Deon, 6. Halterman appeared on TLC's "1000-Lb. Sisters" from the reality show's inception in 2020 until his exit in 2023.
